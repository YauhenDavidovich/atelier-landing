Rules that matter on a dark page:

- Shoot in diffuse daylight, never under a yellow bulb. Grey card for white balance.
- No flash, no frame, no glass.
- Crop to the canvas edge. Any wall or floor around it reads as amateur.
- Keep a second frame of the paint surface up close — texture sells oil better than the whole picture.
